House rules read me

  1. i.Bring something you made. Not links to other people's work.
  2. ii.No takedowns. Critique a piece, never a person.
  3. iii.Drafts are welcome. Mark them <draft> so the room knows.
  4. iv.The Friday open mic is sacred. Show up early or stay late.
  5. v.Be a person. If a thread starts to bite, host steps in.
